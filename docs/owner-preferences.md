# Owner preferences

The app owner's known taste. In an autonomous run (overnight, "don't ask,
decide"), a UX choice that goes against this list, or that the design shows
but this list doesn't cover, is **listed for the owner instead of built**.

Fill this per app and keep it short. Examples from earlier apps:

- Prices on add-to-cart show unit price × quantity.
- No looping decorative animations (sheens, auto-playing carousels) unless asked.
- Banners are images only, unless asked otherwise.
